Dr Sakshi Batra

MBBS, MD Pediatrics, DM Neonatology

Dr Sakshi Batra is a Pediatrician and Neonatologist with over 9 years of experience in newborn and child healthcare. She completed her MBBS from UCMS & GTB Hospital, MD in Pediatrics from LHMC & Kalawati Saran Children’s Hospital, and DM in Neonatology from Dr Ram Manohar Lohia Hospital, New Delhi.

Her expertise includes care of high-risk and preterm newborns, neonatal intensive care, and advanced respiratory support.

She is an active member of the Indian Academy of Pediatrics (IAP) and the National Neonatology Forum (NNF).
